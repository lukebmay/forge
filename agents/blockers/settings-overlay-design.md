## Why this is human-only

Only you can decide whether Forge settings should move from the
extension prefs window into an in-shell overlay menu.

## What the human must do

The shortcuts cheatsheet can already be an interactive overlay
(scroll + collapsible headings). That does **not** mean settings
should live there.

1. When you have time, schedule a short design meeting:
   - [ ] Keep settings in GNOME extension prefs (status quo)
   - [ ] Or sketch an in-shell settings overlay (what pages, what
         stays in prefs, how it opens)
1. Tick a choice (or “later”) when you have decided:
   - [ ] Prefs stay as they are
   - [ ] Design a settings overlay (follow-up plan after the meeting)
   - [ ] Later — leave this reminder

Do not expect agents to build a settings overlay until this meeting
lands a lock.
